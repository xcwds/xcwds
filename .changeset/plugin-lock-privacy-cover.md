New `@xcwds/plugin-lock`: on private pages (`private: true` routes), a cover over the app in the app switcher and no spellcheck, autocomplete or autocapitalize in text fields, with a "Cover private pages in the app switcher" switch in Settings → Privacy. `@xcwds/plugin-settings` shows fields in the `privacy` section inside its Privacy card, and `xcwds add lock` adds the plugin.
