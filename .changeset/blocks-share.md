Share an app's design: Share design (on Your apps and in the builder) offers a link, Copy link and Save as file, with the app's blocks but never what's saved in them, and asks first for a private app. `<ImportApp>` at `<path>/import` opens a shared link or file, lists every block and field, and adds a copy on Add app. The design travels in the link's fragment, compressed, so it never reaches a server.
