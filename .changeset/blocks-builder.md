Make your own apps: `<AppBuilder>` (at `<path>/edit` and, for private apps, `<path>/private-edit`) builds a new app from checklists, counters, logs and notes, or edits one, asking before a change removes saved data. Your apps gets New app, an app's page gets Edit app, and `app.blocks.editPathOf` gives the builder's page.
